Vidyam AI — Building the AI Operating System for Education

AI-native copilots for students and educators powered by an AI Teacher Brain.

School Copilot — www.schoolcopilot.ai

An AI copilot designed to power schools through intelligent academic workflows. Guided by pedagogy and learning science, it helps generate exam papers and quizzes (online/offline), evaluate answer sheets (objective/subjective), and create study resources, teaching material, and homework, while enabling fun and interactive learning through our conversational AI, Mai.

Student Copilot — Saarthi  Try on WhatsApp at 7678910123

A personalized AI learning companion that adapts to each student's class, pace, and understanding. Guided by pedagogy and the goals of the curriculum, Saarthi helps students grasp concepts through relatable examples, solve doubts, and practice with personalized quizzes, bridging learning gaps and making quality learning accessible to every student.

Together, these copilots create a connected learning ecosystem that empowers schools and helps every student learn the way education is meant to be experienced.

Vidyam LLMs: Precision Learning for Every Grade

General-purpose LLMs like ChatGPT often miss the mark in delivering precise educational content tailored to specific curricula. Vidyam LLM bridges this gap by providing clear, curriculum-aligned responses for every grade level.

Whether you're in class 6 or class 12, Vidyam LLM ensures that you receive explanations that are directly aligned with your textbooks and syllabus.

Vidyam LLM: Key Characteristics

Curriculum-Specific Models

Vidyam LLM is fine-tuned for the NCERT syllabus, ensuring relevance and accuracy across all subjects and grade levels.

Subject-Focused

Initially focused on Science for classes 6–12, with future plans to expand to other subjects and curricula.

Multilingual Support

While currently available in English, we aim to support multiple Indian languages soon to reach every student.

Grade-Appropriate Responses

The models provide explanations and examples tailored to the students' age and grade level for better understanding.
